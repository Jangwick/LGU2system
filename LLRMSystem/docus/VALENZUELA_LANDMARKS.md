# Valenzuela City Landmarks & Infrastructure

The City Government of Valenzuela is committed to sustainable development and cultural preservation. Below are key landmarks and infrastructure projects:

## Featured Infrastructure

### Valenzuela People's Park
- **Category**: Recreation
- **Description**: A 1.5-hectare urban park featuring a mini-zoo, dancing fountain, children's playground, and ample green space for families and fitness enthusiasts.
- **Features**: Interactive Fountain, Amphitheater, Aero Circle.

### Disiplina Village
- **Category**: Housing
- **Description**: The country's biggest in-city resettlement site, providing safe and decent homes to thousands of families previously living in danger zones.
- **Features**: 100+ Buildings, School & Health Centers, Livelihood Training Center.

### New Legislative Building
- **Category**: Government
- **Description**: A modern facility housing the sessions of the City Council, providing a transparent and efficient environment for local legislation.

### Flood Control Infrastructure
- **Category**: Resilience
- **Description**: Advanced pumping stations and catchment basins to mitigate flooding and ensure safety, specifically the Paspas Flood Control series.

## City Landmarks (Heritage & Progress)

### Polo Riverwalk Phase 1
- **Category**: TOURISM
- **Status**: New Opening
- **Description**: A 6-kilometer linear park featuring walking paths and cycle lanes along the Polo River, connecting multiple barangays.

### Sentro Health Hubs
- **Category**: HEALTHCARE
- **Status**: Inaugurated
- **Description**: State-of-the-art community health centers in Karuhatan and Canumay West offering specialized diagnostic services.

### Tagalag Fishing Village
- **Category**: ECO-TOURISM
- **Status**: Heritage Site
- **Description**: The city's premier eco-tourism destination, promoting sustainable livelihood and cultural tradition.

### San Diego De Alcala
- **Category**: Heritage
- **Description**: 17th-century historical belfry standing as a witness to the city's rich past.

### Museo ng Valenzuela
- **Category**: Heritage
- **Description**: Repository of the city's historical and cultural heritage.

### WES Arena
- **Category**: Sports/Development
- **Description**: State-of-the-art sports and multi-purpose indoor facility.
